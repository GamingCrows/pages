Answer The Signal

Three years ago your wingmate flew toward a mysterious signal and never came back. Now the signal is back, and it is using her voice.

⚡

Graze & Overdrive

Skim enemy bullets to fill your Overdrive meter, then unleash it: time slows, your weapon transforms and every bullet on screen turns into points.

🚀

5 Weapons, 3 Levels Each

Blaster, Spread, Laser, Homing Missiles and bouncing Orbs, each with its own Overdrive form. Charge a piercing shot or clear the screen with a Nova bomb.

👾

Giant Bosses

5 giant bosses and 5 mini-bosses with multi-phase attacks, telegraphed mega beams and bullet patterns. Bring them down to earn new weapons.

🌌

5 Hostile Sectors

An infested asteroid belt, a frozen nebula, a spore-choked jungle planet, a machine fortress... and whatever waits beyond.

🎬

A Cinematic Story

Animated talking portraits, in-flight radio calls and cutscenes. Hidden Black Boxes wait in every stage for the pilots who look closely.

🎮

5 Ways To Play

Story, Arcade, the ever-changing Daily Signal, plus unlockable Boss Rush and Void Mode.

📱

Built For Mobile

Floating or fixed joystick, auto-fire, left-handed layout, reduced flashing option, plus gamepad and keyboard support.

📡

Fully Offline

No account, no sign-in, no internet needed. Ads never appear during gameplay or cutscenes.
